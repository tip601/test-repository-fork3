I am the developer of GroupMaker, a teaching management system configured as a no-formalities, freely available Windows application installable from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NK50R9QSMFR). Anyone can register as a student or an instructor, or both, fictitious identities acceptable. Those registering as instructors can reserve space for their classrooms and specify an access code that their students must enter when they register to join the class. Reservations are good for 30 days, but can be renewed without limit.
